# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Listeners, artists, vocalists, rappers, music supervisors, and collaborators who discover blnfkr through social links, streaming platforms, beat snippets, and visual identity. They arrive quickly, often from mobile, and need to understand the sound, credibility, credits, and where to listen or contact.

## Product Purpose

This site is a compact artist presence for blnfkr, a young but experienced music producer and beatmaker. It should promote tracks, Apple Music, YouTube, Linktree, and Instagram while turning the album-cover style into a memorable visual world.

Success means a visitor immediately remembers the name, hears the producer identity, trusts the credits, and moves to Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, or Linktree.

## Brand Personality

Nocturnal, distorted, self-made.

The voice is direct and personal: hobby-driven over polished industry theater, but with enough credits and radio history to feel credible. The visual system should feel like cover art, club lighting, screenshots, and rough digital print texture rather than a generic music landing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid clean corporate music portfolios, beige creator templates, generic studio websites, fake luxury, overexplained personal branding, soft pastel musician pages, and social-button grids without atmosphere.

Avoid using the artist's provocative handle as a cheap shock device. It should appear where links or social identity require it, while the main artist name stays sharp and easy to remember.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the artifact: the first screen should feel like entering an album-cover world, not reading a biography.
2. Make the exit routes obvious: Apple Music, YouTube, Instagram, and Linktree must be visible and usable.
3. Prove experience without overexplaining: credits, radio notes, and years of practice should read as evidence inside the story.
4. Keep the surface raw but controlled: use distortion, noise, and harsh contrast with enough structure for mobile readability.
5. Treat images as identity: the covers and background sketch define color, rhythm, and atmosphere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, readable contrast over imagery, clear focus states, descriptive alt text, and reduced-motion support. The site should work on mobile without requiring hover or precise pointer control.
